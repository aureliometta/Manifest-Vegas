Good to Know
🥂
What Manifest Covers
Gas for the drive, all alcohol over the weekend, and Saturday brunch. That's it — and it's a lot. Dinners, activities, and everything else are on you. Budget accordingly before you leave Irvine.
🚗
Rides
All carpools are organized in the ride sheet. Most people depart Friday at 9:00 AM sharp — if you have a non-negotiable conflict, there is one afternoon car available. Reach out in the group chat to claim a spot.
🎰
Gambling
You're in Vegas — if you want to gamble, go for it. Set a limit before you walk onto the floor and do not go over it. The casino always wins. It's more fun when you treat it like entertainment, not a strategy.
💧
Drink Smart
Vegas is hot and dry. Hydrate between drinks. Alcohol hits different when you're dehydrated and on your feet all day. Pace yourself — especially Day 1, when you've got a full Saturday ahead.
📍
Hotel Address
Otonomus Hotel Las Vegas — 5050 W Russell Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89118. Save this to your maps now. It's about 10–15 min from the Strip — Ubers are cheap and easy.
